## About
Rob Lach is an independent game developer known for his unique approach to game design. He gained recognition for developing "POP: Methodology Experiment One," a game that blends experimental design with a focus on music and artistic expression. Lach's process is unconventional—he first created the soundtrack and then built the gameplay mechanics around it. He emphasizes creative freedom in game development and is passionate about exploring new ways to engage players emotionally. His work stands out for its artistic vision, merging music, visuals, and gameplay into a cohesive experience.
