## About
Windy Gaming was started 6 1/2 years ago by Chris Dammacco with the goal of providing video games and accessories at reasonable prices while treating customers as they wanted to be treated. Over the years what the store specialized in has changed from being an import shop dealing in import home video games, into imported arcade cabinets, and finally into their own line of original products.

Unfortunately Windy Gaming has been shut down since January 2020.
