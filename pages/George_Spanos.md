## About
George Spanos has traveled all over to repair arcade machines. Spanos is a freelance technician that goes around the Chicago suburbs at arcades or warehouses and fixes whatever he can. At a young age he loved arcade machines and as he went to college he wanted to keep working with them and started to be a freelancer. Helping a number of arcades with restoring their machines.
