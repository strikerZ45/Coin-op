# George Spanos
<img src="https://strikerz45.github.io/Coin-op/objects/spanos01.jpg" alt="George Spanos"  height="450">

Speaking of repairing old machines, no one has traveled all over to repair arcade machines like George Spanos. Spanos is a freelance technician that goes around the Chicago suburbs at arcades or warehouses and fixes whatever he can. At a young age he loved arcade machines and as he went to college he wanted to keep working with them and started to be a freelancer. Helping a number of arcades with restoring their machines.
