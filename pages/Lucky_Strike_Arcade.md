# Lucky Strike Arcade
<img src="https://strikerz45.github.io/Coin-op/objects/Front_Sign.jpg" alt="Front of building"  height="500">

Lucky Strike FTW ( For the Win ) arcade is located in Streeterville, Chicago and opened in 2015. It is home to about 130 arcade games, and a backlit bowling alley, great for the whole family to enjoy, but also has a sports bar and pool tables to cater to young adults. It was built as an expansion of the existing Lucky Strike bowling alley to offer a more immersive entertainment experience. Lucky Strike FTW arcade is desgined for experiencing quality time for get-togethers with friends and families, with their variety of party bundle options.
