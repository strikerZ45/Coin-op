# Midwest Repair 
<img src="https://strikerz45.github.io/Coin-op/objects/Broken_Pinball_Machine_02.JPG" alt="Broken Pinball1"  height="450">

Midwest is a small privately owned shop that does repair work to pinball machines in the Chicago area. The shop is more suitable for residential customers because the shop does not work on commercial units. 

## Rich Huff
<img src="https://strikerz45.github.io/Coin-op/objects/Rich_Huff.JPG" alt="Rich Huff"  height="450">

Rich Huff started his business when he was a part time pinball repairman and also worked as a business consultant. The shop services any type of pinball machine because Rich has experience on working on the earliest pinball machines all the way up to the modern ones. 

## George Spanos
<img src="https://strikerz45.github.io/Coin-op/objects/spanos01.jpg" alt="George Spanos"  height="450">

Speaking of repairing old machines, no one has traveled all over to repair arcade machines like George Spanos. Spanos is a freelance technician that goes around the Chicago suburbs at arcades or warehouses and fixes whatever he can. At a young age he loved arcade machines and as he went to college he wanted to keep working with them and started to be a freelancer. Helping a number of arcades with restoring their machines.
