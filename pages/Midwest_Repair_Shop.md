# Midwest Repair 
<img src="https://strikerz45.github.io/Coin-op/objects/Broken_Pinball_Machine_02.JPG" alt="Broken Pinball1"  height="450">

Midwest is a small privately owned shop that does repair work to pinball machines in the Chicago area. The shop is more suitable for residential customers because the shop does not work on commercial units.
