## About
Doc Mack is the owner of Galloping Ghost, which currently holds the title of the largest arcade in the world. Every year his collections slows growing larger and this is how he started Galloping Ghost. From the early years of the arcade, it was only home to Mack's few personal arcade machines. Mack would go online to search for new release, high demand or games that he thinks gamers would enjoy in his arcade. The arcade works with an entry fee and then all the games are free to play, making his business coinless and suitable for anyone.
